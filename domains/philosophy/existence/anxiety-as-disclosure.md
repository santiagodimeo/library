# Anxiety as disclosure

Kierkegaard's Vigilius Haufniensis defines anxiety as "the dizziness of freedom, which emerges when the spirit wants to posit the synthesis" (CA 61), and holds that it "is not merely a pathological condition which is a symptom of disease, but part of the human condition itself. It is an awareness of freedom" (SEP, Kierkegaard). Its structure is a "sympathetic antipathy" — simultaneous attraction to and repulsion from one's own possibility. The normative conclusion is not removal: "whoever has learned to be anxious in the right way has learned the ultimate" (CA 155).

Sartre inherits the disclosure claim. Anxiety "discloses this predicament to me, revealing that I've been abandoned to a realm of possibilities", with "no excuses behind us nor justifications before us"; bad faith is the refusal of this, denying or over-identifying with either facticity or transcendence (SEP, Existentialism). The standing objection is that if the self is always self-making, "true to ourselves" has no fixed referent — to which Sartre and Beauvoir answer that authenticity means accepting the instability itself.

Two adjacent doctrines are routinely flattened in popular use. Epictetus does not say externals are merely hard to control: body, possessions, relationships, reputation and outcomes are *indifferent in value*, a metaphysical claim about what constitutes the self, not stress-management advice (SEP, Epictetus). And reading *wu wei* as "do nothing" is, per SEP, a Neo-Confucian misreading; Daoism prescribes practised virtuosity and spontaneity (*ziran*) aligned with circumstance, not passivity (Hansen, Daoism).

## Tradeoffs

These are positions, not findings, and the tradition disagrees internally about whether anxiety is primarily about death, freedom, or meaning.

The clinical translation is weaker than the philosophy. Existential psychotherapy's controlled evidence sits almost entirely in advanced-cancer and palliative populations, and short-term gains largely faded by two years. Treat the philosophical claim as a frame that happens to converge with what detached mindfulness asks behaviourally — see [[verification-as-compulsion]] — not as evidence that the frame is therapeutic.
